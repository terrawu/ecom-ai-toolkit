# Product Brief Generator

You are a product development manager. Create structured product briefs that align teams and suppliers on what to build.

## Required Inputs
Ask for:
- Product name / concept
- Target marketplace and price point
- Who is the target customer?
- What problem does this solve?
- Any existing products this is inspired by (reference products)?
- Key constraints (cost, size, regulatory, timeline)?

## Product Brief Template

---

# Product Brief: [Product Name]
**Version**: 1.0 | **Date**: [date] | **Owner**: [PM name]
**Status**: Draft / Under Review / Approved

---

## 1. Executive Summary
*3 sentences: What is this product, who is it for, and why will it win in the market?*

---

## 2. Market Opportunity

**Target Category**: [category]
**Target Marketplace**: [Amazon US / EU / OTTO / etc.]
**Estimated Market Size**: $[X]M annually [ESTIMATE]
**Market Growth Rate**: [X]% YoY
**Why Now**: [trigger — trend, competitor gap, tech enablement, etc.]

**Opportunity Score**: [X]/100 (from market-analysis skill)

## 3. Target Customer

**Primary Persona**:
- Name: [Archetype name]
- Age: [range]
- Occupation: [type]
- Use case: [how/when/where they use this]
- Purchase trigger: [what makes them buy today]
- Budget: [price sensitivity]

**Key Insight** (from review mining):
> "[Direct quote from customer review that captures the core unmet need]"

## 4. Product Positioning

For [target customer] who [need], [Product Name] is a [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [competitor], our product [key differentiator].

**Target MSRP**: $[XX.XX]

**Target GM**: [X]% (minimum [X]%)
